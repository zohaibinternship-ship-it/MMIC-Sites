Who We Are

XEMMIC operates at the intersection of geological science and strategic investment, bringing Pakistan's vast mineral potential to the global stage.

As part of AmanorX Holdings, we combine deep local knowledge with international best practices to evaluate, manage, and market mineral and metals exploration projects across Pakistan's most promising territories.

Evaluation

Comprehensive geological assessment and resource quantification across diverse terrains and mineral types — from preliminary surveys to detailed feasibility studies.

Management

End-to-end project management from initial survey through extraction planning — ensuring operational excellence, safety, and regulatory compliance at every phase.

Marketing

Strategic positioning of mineral assets in domestic and international markets — connecting producers with the right buyers through data-driven market intelligence.

Investment

Connecting mineral opportunities with strategic capital for sustainable, value-driven development — structuring deals that serve all stakeholders equitably.
