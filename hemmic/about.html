About HEMMIC

Healthcare evaluation, management, marketing and investment

HEMMIC is AmanorX Holdings' dedicated healthcare arm. We evaluate, manage, market, and invest in healthcare opportunities across Pakistan.

Our mission is to build a healthier Pakistan by identifying and nurturing the most promising healthcare ventures. We combine deep industry expertise with strategic capital to help healthcare businesses grow and scale.

As part of AmanorX Holdings, we share a commitment to excellence, integrity, and long-term value creation.
