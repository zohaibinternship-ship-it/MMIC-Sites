Corporate Pillar

Strategic Altitude

AVEMMIC is AmanorX Holdings’ dedicated aviation arm. We evaluate, manage, market, and invest in aviation opportunities across Pakistan — engineering the systems, capital and partnerships that carry the sector to its next era of flight.

Network Reliability

From route economics to ground systems and market access, we are committed to building a resilient, well-engineered aviation network that lifts connectivity and confidence across the country.

Generational Foundation

As a foundational asset of AmanorX Holdings, we build for the long haul — durable infrastructure, disciplined investment, and generational value that keeps the next era of flight within reach.
