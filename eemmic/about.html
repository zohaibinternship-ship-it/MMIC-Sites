About EEMMIC

Powering Pakistan's energy future

EEMMIC is AmanorX Holdings' dedicated energy arm. We evaluate, manage, market, and invest in energy opportunities across Pakistan.

Our mission is to power Pakistan's future by identifying and nurturing the most promising energy ventures. We combine deep industry expertise with strategic capital to help energy businesses grow and scale.

From renewable energy to traditional power, we're committed to building a sustainable, energy-secure Pakistan.

As part of AmanorX Holdings, we share a commitment to excellence, integrity, and long-term value creation.
