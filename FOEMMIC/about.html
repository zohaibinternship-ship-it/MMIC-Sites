About FOEMMIC

From source to table

Corporate Pillar

A Considered Table

FOEMMIC is AmanorX Holdings’ dedicated food arm. We evaluate, manage, market, and invest in food opportunities across Pakistan — building considered, quality-first ventures that carry the sector from source to table.

Our Core Mission

To shape the future of food in Pakistan by identifying, evaluating and scaling high-quality ventures — connecting growers, kitchens, brands and capital with care and a single, considered standard.

Source to Table

From sourcing and supply chains to brand and distribution, we are committed to building a warm, dependable food network that lifts quality and trust across the country.

Generational Foundation

As a foundational asset of AmanorX Holdings, we build for the long term — durable supply, disciplined investment, and generational value that keeps good food within reach for everyone.
